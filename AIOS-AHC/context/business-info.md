## Organization Overview

Affordable House Corp is a residential builder specializing in turnkey construction of investment properties for property investor clients. The company has been established for 30 years, currently turns over circa $100m, and employs a team of 50. It is the market leader in rooming accommodation construction.

## Products / Services / Focus Areas

- Rooming accommodation (market-leading specialty)
- Co-living developments
- Dual occupancy properties
- Boutique townhouses
- Land subdivisions
- Pre-fabricated houses
- Full turnkey service: feasibility testing, land acquisition, construction, and final sale

## Key Context

- **Stage:** Established, mature operation (30 years in market)
- **Team:** 50 staff
- **Revenue:** Circa $100m annual turnover
- **Track record:** 2,100+ projects delivered, $945m+ total project value, 8.2% average gross yield, 99% on-time completion
- **Business model:** Construction project-based (not recurring revenue)
- **Service area:** South East Queensland (Brisbane and surrounds)
- **Customer acquisition:** Primarily repeat business and referrals. The company is currently investing in a more comprehensive website to attract new clients beyond its existing referral network.
- **Competitive differentiation:** Longevity and reputation as market leader in rooming accommodation; end-to-end turnkey offering spanning feasibility through to sale — most competitors don't cover the full pipeline.
- **Current initiative:** Marketing overhaul in progress — new website (with build calculator and colour selection tool, SEO-optimized) and refreshed brochures, aimed at presenting a more professional, modern image against competitors.
